## Summary

The nightshift-cart Lambda function began receiving AccessDeniedException errors after a deployment changed the CART_TABLE_NAME environment variable. The IAM role for nightshift-cart lacks the dynamodb:PutItem permission for the new table or its ARN, causing all write operations to fail.
